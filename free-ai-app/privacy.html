Privacy Policy — Free AI Chat

Last updated: 20 November 2026. Free AI Chat is a free AI chat website. This page explains what data the site accesses when you choose to connect your Google account, and how that data is used.

Google account connection (optional)

Connecting Google is optional. If you tap "Gmail jodo" / connect Google, Google asks for your permission for three things:

  • Read your Gmail (gmail.readonly) — so the chat can show your emails to you when you ask, e.g. "meri email dikhao".
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) — so the chat can send an email only after you approve the draft by saying "bhej do". Nothing is sent without your approval.
  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload) — so a video you pick on your phone can be uploaded to your own channel, with the title/description you approve.

Each user connects their own Google account. Your Gmail and your YouTube channel stay yours — uploads go to your channel, emails are read from and sent from your account.

How your Google data is handled

  • The Google access token is kept only in your browser's memory for about one hour and is not stored on our server. When it expires, Google asks you to Allow again.
  • Your emails are shown to you in the chat. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your Gmail or YouTube data for advertising.
  • Video uploads go directly from your browser to Google/YouTube using Google's upload service.
  • We do not share your Google data with other users. Other people using Free AI Chat cannot see your emails or your channel.

Chat data

Chat messages are sent to the AI service that generates replies. If you use the "Chat hamesha save karo" (permanent chat) feature with Google login or your own code, your chat history is saved under your account/code so you can continue later. Files you attach (photos, PDFs, text) are used only to answer your question about them.

Removing access

You can remove Free AI Chat's access at any time from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access → Free AI Chat → Remove access. After removal, the chat can no longer read your Gmail, send email, or upload to YouTube for you.
